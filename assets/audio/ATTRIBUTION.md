# VoiceBank comparison samples

These three files are the matching `p232_001.wav` utterance extracted from the University of Edinburgh test archives:

- `voicebank-p232_001-clean.wav` — `clean_testset_wav.zip`
- `voicebank-p232_001-noisy.wav` — `noisy_testset_wav.zip`
- `voicebank-p232_001-reverb.wav` — `reverb_testset_wav.zip`

Source datasets:

- Cassia Valentini-Botinhao, *Noisy speech database for training speech enhancement algorithms and TTS models* (2017), University of Edinburgh, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/2117
- Cassia Valentini-Botinhao, *Reverberant speech database for training speech dereverberation algorithms and TTS models* (2016), University of Edinburgh, https://doi.org/10.7488/ds/1425

License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Changes: the WAV payloads were extracted from their ZIP archives and renamed for a compact browser A/B exercise. The audio samples themselves were not trimmed, resampled, normalized, or otherwise altered.

`DeNoise` and `DeReverb` remain reference-only in the lab. These files provide matched material for listening and future algorithm validation; their presence does not imply that either Premiere effect has been reproduced.
